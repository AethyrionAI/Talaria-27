Privacy Policy

Talaria for iOS · Effective: 2026-08-20

The short version: Talaria has no servers. We — the app's developer — do not collect, receive, store, sell, or share any of your data. There are no accounts, no analytics, and no tracking. Your conversations are processed on your iPhone by default, on Apple's Private Cloud Compute if you choose that option, or on a computer you own and operate. The only parties that ever handle your data are Apple — its on-device frameworks, and Private Cloud Compute if you enable it — and the services you yourself configure.

Who we are

Talaria is an iOS client for a personal AI assistant. It is developed by James Jones. This policy covers the Talaria iOS app and this website.

The three ways Talaria works

On-device (the default). Out of the box, Talaria's assistant runs entirely on your iPhone using Apple's on-device foundation models. Your messages, attachments, and the assistant's replies are processed and stored on your device. Nothing is sent to us — or to anyone.

Apple's Private Cloud Compute (optional, beta). Some requests need a larger model than fits on the phone. If you select Private Cloud β as the chat brain, those requests are sent to Apple's Private Cloud Compute — Apple silicon servers — instead of being processed on your iPhone. Your request leaves the device. It does not come to us: we have no servers and no visibility into it.

Apple describes the handling of that data as follows, as published at apple.com/privacy on 2026-08-20: the data required to fulfil your request is sent to Apple silicon servers and “no other data is sent”; the request is processed and is “never stored or accessible to Apple”; and Apple states that this promise “can be verified by independent experts.” Those are Apple's commitments, quoted rather than paraphrased, and they are Apple's to keep. We are telling you where your data goes; we are not making a promise about what happens to it there.

Paired with your own server (optional). You can connect Talaria to a Hermes agent running on your own computer. In that mode, the content you send — messages, attachments, and voice — travels over your own private network (for example, a Tailscale tailnet you operate) to your own machine. That machine, and any AI model providers you have configured on it under your own accounts, process your data under your control and those providers' own terms. We are not a party to any of it.

What the app stores on your device

Conversation history, attachments you add, settings, and pairing credentials (kept in the iOS Keychain) are stored locally on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes this local data. Talaria does not sync your data to iCloud or to any cloud service of ours.

Voice

Talaria's default voice mode uses Apple's on-device speech recognition and synthesis. What you say is still answered by whichever brain you have selected — so if you have chosen Private Cloud β, your transcribed words are sent to Apple's Private Cloud Compute exactly as a typed message would be. When paired with your own server, you can optionally use a realtime voice engine: in that mode your audio is streamed through your server to the voice provider you configured there (for example, OpenAI's realtime API under your own API key). This is off unless you configure it yourself on your own server. When a voice session is running on that realtime engine, the app shows an indicator on the voice screen for the whole session, so you can tell at the time that your audio is leaving the phone.

What we receive: nothing

The app contains no analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDKs, and makes no network connections to infrastructure we operate — because we operate none. If you choose to share diagnostics with developers via iOS settings, Apple may provide us aggregate crash reports through App Store Connect under Apple's privacy terms.

Third parties, exhaustively

  • Apple — on-device models, Speech, WeatherKit, HealthKit, and Private Cloud Compute (optional, off unless you select it), each under Apple's privacy commitments.
  • Your own server and the providers you configured on it — under your control and their terms, not ours.
  • This website is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads fonts from Google Fonts; both services receive standard request logs (IP address, user agent). The site itself sets no cookies and runs no analytics.

Your data, your controls

Delete a conversation in the app to remove it locally; delete the app to remove everything local; unpair to stop all traffic to your server. Data on your own server is yours to manage there. Since we hold nothing, there is nothing for you to request from us — but questions are welcome at the address below.

Children

Talaria is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone — children included, by construction.

Changes

If the app's data practices ever change, this policy will be updated before the change ships, with the effective date above revised.
